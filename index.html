★ ABOUT THE GAME ★RAMANATHAPURAM
A race the world
hasn't heard of

On the Ramanathapuram coast there’s a race that most of the world has never heard of. Fishing villages build wooden sailboats by hand. They crew them with six men who read the sea the way other people read a clock, and once a year they race them, with the whole shore on the sand shouting their names.

THE MAKER

Developed by Vishva Dev. The boats, the sea, the village, the crew and the sailing simulation were all crafted and built with passion and precision. This is version 1: more of the race, the boats and the village will be improved in future versions.

PLEASE NOTE

This is an independent, non-commercial fan tribute. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the makers of the film Mandaadi, its producers, cast or crew. The film’s name, story and characters belong to their owners. The village, crew and events in this game are fictional, and any resemblance to real people is coincidental. The story clips were made with AI image and video tools.

The sailing and the controls are simplified for play. Real paaimaram racing is far richer: tides and currents, the feel of the hull, the crew’s calls, and a craft that takes a lifetime to learn. Please don’t take a real paaimaram out on what you learned here.
